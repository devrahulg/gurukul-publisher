theAIgurukul

theAIgurukul is a free, daily short-video series that teaches Kiro, AWS's agentic IDE, in English and Hindi.

Episodes are published on YouTube and Instagram. Gurukul Publisher is the small internal tool that uploads and schedules those episodes to theAIgurukul's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not sign in anyone other than the channel owner.
